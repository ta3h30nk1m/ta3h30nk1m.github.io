Taeheon Kim

I am a 3rd-year Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Machine Perception and Reasoning Lab, Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Jonghyun Choi.

My research focuses on model post-training through efficient learning algorithms, including continual learning, domain adaptation, machine unlearning, and knowledge transfer such as federated learning and model merging.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Yonsei University.
